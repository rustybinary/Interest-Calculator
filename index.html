Rules used

Banks do not deduct TDS from savings account interest. Interest on fixed and recurring deposits is subject to TDS once it passes ₹50,000 a year at one bank (₹1,00,000 for senior citizens), at 10%, or 20% without a valid PAN. FD and RD interest at the same bank is added together. The section is 393(1) under the Income-tax Act 2025, formerly 194A. Form 121 replaces Forms 15G and 15H from FY 2026-27. Slabs shown are for FY 2026-27 and are applied to every year. Surcharge is not included. Fixed deposit interest is counted day by day, on a 366-day basis in financial years with 29 February and 365 otherwise, and rounded to the rupee at each credit; both can be switched off under Edit rate card.

Sources: TaxGarden, AU Small Finance Bank, Tax2win, ClearTax.
